One score from three independent signals.

Every stock gets a 0–100 score for trend, relative strength and momentum from adjusted end-of-day bars. The composite is their plain average, so it rewards stocks where all three line up today. Scans then say which specific setup, if any, is present.

Scan playbook

Every scan has one job.

Work from leadership state to watch, setup and entry trigger. Flow scans look for durable leadership held for months; Burst scans look for a fast move over one to twenty sessions.

The composite

Score = ( Trend + Relative strength + Momentum ) ÷ 3, rounded

It describes condition, not odds. A 90 means trend, relative strength and momentum are all strongly aligned right now. It does not mean a 90% chance of going up. A scan match is still what says whether a complete setup or trigger exists.

Data and exclusions

  • Universe: Russell 1000, S&P 400 and S&P 600 members. Benchmark: IWB (Russell 1000 ETF, dividends included).
  • Bars are daily, adjusted for splits and dividends, from Yahoo Finance. Short interest is exchange-reported twice a month, so it lags.
  • A stock is left unscored when it has under 260 sessions of history, has no bar for the latest session, or shows a one-day jump that looks like a bad adjustment.

Where this page fills in the blanks

The scoring weights and scan thresholds follow the published rules. Several terms in those rules have no published definition, so this scanner uses its own. They are settings at the top of scripts/composite_scan.py and easy to change:

  • Rising average: the 50-day is higher than 10 sessions ago; the 200-day higher than 20 sessions ago.
  • ATR extension: distance above the 20-day average, in 14-day ATRs.
  • RS acceleration: 3-month excess return minus half the 6-month excess return.
  • 55-day pivot: highest high of the prior 55 sessions. A break is "fresh" when no close cleared its own pivot in the 10 sessions before.
  • Contracting range and volume: last 10 sessions' average true range ≤ 85%, and volume ≤ 90%, of the 30 sessions before them.
  • Prior 6-month leadership: at least 10 points ahead of the benchmark over 6 months, with a 52-week high in the last 63 sessions.
  • Near an average: from 0.5 ATR below to 1 ATR above it. Strong close: top 35% of the day's range. Weak close: bottom 35%.
  • Gap retained: today's close relative to the gap open, measured from the pre-gap close.
  • Industry rank: percentile of the industry's median RS score among all industries. Peer rank: the stock's RS percentile inside its industry.
Because of those choices, matches here will not be identical to any other scanner built on the same published rules. Treat the lists as a starting point for chart review.
